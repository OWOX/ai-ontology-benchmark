# 4x4

The 4x4 is a [cube](../definitions.md#puzzles) with four layers along each axis, so it has 8 corners, 24 edge pieces, 24 centre pieces, and 96 stickers on six faces. No centre is fixed. Its [moves](../definitions.md#puzzles) are quarter and half turns of a face and of the two outer layers on a side, the [wide turns](../definitions.md#puzzles), written in WCA [notation](../definitions.md#puzzles) as `R`, `U'`, `F2`, `Rw`, `Uw'`, and `Fw2`.

Its [corruptions](../definitions.md#puzzles) are `twisted corner`, `flipped wing`, and `recoloured sticker`, never on a centre sticker. A swap of two pieces can be reachable on the 4x4, so it isn't one.
