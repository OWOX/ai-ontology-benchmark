# 5x5

The 5x5 is a [cube](../definitions.md#puzzles) with five layers along each axis, so it has 8 corners, 36 edge pieces, 6 fixed centres, 48 other centre pieces, and 150 stickers on six faces. Its [moves](../definitions.md#puzzles) are quarter and half turns of a face and of the two outer layers on a side, the [wide turns](../definitions.md#puzzles), written in WCA [notation](../definitions.md#puzzles) as `R`, `U'`, `F2`, `Rw`, `Uw'`, and `Fw2`. The middle layer never turns.
