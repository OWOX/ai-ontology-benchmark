# 2x2

The 2x2 is a [cube](../definitions.md#puzzles) with two layers along each axis, so it has 8 corner pieces, no edges or centres, and 24 stickers on six faces. Its [moves](../definitions.md#puzzles) are quarter and half turns of a face, written in Singmaster [notation](../definitions.md#puzzles) as `R`, `U'`, and `F2`.

Its [corruptions](../definitions.md#puzzles) are `twisted corner` and `recoloured sticker`. A swap of two corners stays reachable, so it isn't one.
