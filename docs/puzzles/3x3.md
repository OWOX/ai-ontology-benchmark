# 3x3

The 3x3 is a [cube](../definitions.md#puzzles) with three layers along each axis, so it has 8 corners, 12 edges, 6 fixed centres, and 54 stickers on six faces. Its [moves](../definitions.md#puzzles) are quarter and half turns of a face, written in Singmaster [notation](../definitions.md#puzzles) as `R`, `U'`, and `F2`.

Its [corruptions](../definitions.md#puzzles) are `twisted corner`, `flipped edge`, `swapped corners`, `swapped edges`, and `recoloured sticker`.
